Welcome to Osaka, Japan!

Osaka is a vibrant city known for its rich culture, world-famous street food, dazzling nightlife, and historic landmarks like Osaka Castle. As Japan's kitchen and entertainment hub, Osaka blends tradition with modern energy, offering unforgettable experiences for every traveler.

Dotonburi

Osaka's iconic nightlife district, known for bright neon lights, street food like takoyaki, and the famous Glico sign.

Universal Studios Japan

A top theme park in Osaka featuring world-class rides, movie-themed attractions, and popular zones like Super Nintendo World and The Wizarding World of Harry Potter.
